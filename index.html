Research

How should intelligent systems accumulate experience?

I work across agent runtimes, long-horizon state, and the organization of human–AI work. The recurring question is how experience from one task should change what happens on the next.

01

Long-Horizon Agents

State, memory, recovery, and runtime structure for agents that operate across extended tasks and interruptions.

02

Experience & Work Policies

How trajectories, feedback, and reusable skills can reshape the way future tasks are reasoned about, verified, delegated, and stopped.

03

Human–AI Systems

How repeated collaboration changes expertise, supervision, and the distribution of cognitive work between humans and AI.

About

Researching systems that have a history.

I am a computer science researcher at UT Dallas. My work spans language-model agents, scientific automation, retrieval, and long-horizon intelligent systems.

Across these projects, I am increasingly interested in a shared problem: once an AI system has completed many tasks, what exactly should it have learned from them — and how should that experience change the way the next task is organized?
